Discover the Cultural & Scenic Beauty of Sri Lanka

Classic Sri Lanka Tour

Two private, chauffeur-guided itineraries covering temples, tea country, mountains, wildlife and coast. Pick the length that suits your holiday, then message us to lock in your dates.

7 Days / 6 Nights

The Extended Classic Route

Colombo → Kandy → Nuwara Eliya → Ella → Yala → Bentota → Colombo. The full island loop, including a Yala safari and coastal wind-down.

DAY1

Airport / Colombo → Kandy

  • Pinnawala Elephant Orphanage
  • Spice & Herbal Garden
  • Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic
  • Kandy Lake & City Tour
🛏 Overnight stay in Kandy
DAY2

Kandy → Nuwara Eliya

  • Shri Bhakta Hanuman Temple
  • Tea Plantation & Tea Factory Visit
  • Ramboda Waterfall
  • Gregory Lake
  • Hakgala Botanical Garden
🛏 Overnight stay in Nuwara Eliya
DAY3

Nuwara Eliya → Ella

  • Seetha Amman Temple
  • Nine Arch Bridge
  • Little Adam's Peak
  • Ravana Falls
🛏 Overnight stay in Ella
DAY4

Ella → Yala

  • Scenic Train Journey (Ella to Yala area)
  • Yala National Park Safari
🛏 Overnight stay in Yala
DAY5

Yala → Bentota

  • Scenic Southern Drive
  • Relax at Bentota Beach
  • Madu River Boat Safari
  • Optional Water Sports
🛏 Overnight stay in Bentota
DAY6

Bentota → Colombo

  • Turtle Hatchery
  • Colombo City Tour
  • Shopping
🛏 Overnight stay in Colombo
DAY7

Colombo → Airport

  • Leisure Time
  • Last Minute Shopping
  • Airport Drop-off
